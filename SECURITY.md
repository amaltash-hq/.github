# Security

Found a security problem in Amaltash? Thank you for telling us.

## How to report it

Email **support@amaltash.com**. Put "Security" in the subject line.

Please include:

- What you found, and where.
- Steps to show the problem, if you have them.
- How it could affect people who use Amaltash.

Please do not open a public GitHub issue, pull request or discussion about it. Public posts can put people at risk before a fix is ready.

## What happens next

We read every report. We will reply, look into it, and keep you posted while we fix it. Please give us a fair chance to fix the problem before you share it with anyone.

## What this covers

- The calculator server at `https://tools.amaltash.com/mcp`
- The plugins in [amaltash-hq/mcp](https://github.com/amaltash-hq/mcp)
- amaltash.com, trade.amaltash.com and the Amaltash apps
